# Spec 19: path edges, `teach path edges` proposes `after:` lines from what the built lessons use

Round 2 (2026-11-01), at da40b63 (the repo lines this spec names are quoted in snippet 2026-11-01-spec-19-lines-at-research-vault; today's behaviour on the vcluster teach workspace is recorded in experiment 2026-11-01-probe-what-built-vcluster-lessons-introduce, and on the fixture workspace in experiment 2026-11-01-probe-term-tool-use-three-fixture). This is evaluation 16 §4.3 item 2, "Propose term prerequisites, let the author accept", the follow-up spec 17 D5 deferred and spec 20 D6 renamed `teach path edges` (all three quoted in the snippet). Today `after:` is written by hand from the mission interview and never compared with what the lessons do: `pathFringe` reads only each line's `lesson:` and `after:`, the lesson lint checks a lesson's terms and tools against lesson numbers (`introducedTerms` and `taughtTools` take every lesson numbered at or below the one being linted) and never against `after:`, and no command says "this lesson uses a term that line introduces, and its line does not name that line" (snippet 2026-11-01-spec-19-lines-at-research-vault). On the vcluster workspace at da40b63 the three built lessons use no term or tool another line introduces, every line with a term or title use already reaches its introducer, and no attempt has been recorded (experiment 2026-11-01-probe-what-built-vcluster-lessons-introduce, P5 and P6), so the pass proposes nothing there today; its first proposal, if any, comes from a later lesson. This spec adds one read-only verb that derives `after:` proposals from the workspace alone and prints each with the lesson and the term behind it, for the path author to accept or reject. This spec is the executable list for the implementation; nothing here is implemented at da40b63.

## Decisions this spec designs within

Taken in evaluation 16, spec 17 and spec 20 and not reopened here: the graph is the `PATH.md` line graph, authored by the path author; nothing builds a concept graph over the vault's prose (evaluation 16 §4.3, "What not to build"), and no LLM extraction of concepts from the vault's prose, clustering or similarity is used (evaluation 16 §5 "Not adopted" and spec 20 D7, all quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-19-lines-at-research-vault); `after:` means a hard pedagogical prerequisite and nothing else, and a sideways relation (`near:`) is a separate field offered only on a stall (spec 17's decisions paragraph, quoted in the snippet's round 2 additions, which takes the first from the captured note on blocked learners, 2026-11-01-prerequisite-only-paths-block-learners-practitioners, and the second from evaluation 16); machine-proposed prerequisites are proposed, never written, the expert-acceptance pattern the educational-graph note reports (2026-11-01-educational-knowledge-graphs-building-prerequisite-relations); `lesson:` is the state and `teach next`'s `fringe:` and `inner:` lines are unchanged (spec 17 D0 and D1); `teach path candidates` is the line pass from the vault and this is the edge pass from the workspace, the two sharing `readPath` and `missionFloor` and nothing else (spec 20 D6, quoted); and the repo's own rule, "ESM only, no dependencies, no classes" (`AGENTS.md`, quoted).

Four constraints kept throughout: the path author stays the author (proposals are printed, `PATH.md` is never written by the command); the workspace is the only source (no vault read, no fetch, no model call: every proposal is derived from `PATH.md` and `lessons/*.html`); every proposal is auditable (it names the line to change, the value to add, the lesson or title that uses the term or tool, the term or tool itself, and the line that introduces or teaches it); and nothing changes `readPath`'s shape, the lint's violations or exit codes, `pathFringe`, `pathCandidates` or the manifest.

## Problems

1. **`after:` is never compared with what the lessons do.** `pathFringe(lines)` reads `l.n`, `l.lesson` and `l.after` and nothing else (quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-19-lines-at-research-vault); `next` in `bin/commands/teach.mjs` calls `teachIndex` and `pathLines`, which calls `readPath` and `pathFringe` (quoted). The lesson lint's term rule allows a tracked term when the Floor's Known terms or `introducedTerms(ws, id)` hold it, and `introducedTerms` collects `data-introduces` from every lesson whose four-digit number is at or below the lesson's; the tool rule does the same through `taughtTools` (all quoted). So a lesson may use a term introduced by a lesson its line's `after:` does not reach, directly or through the chain, and nothing reports it: the lint passes on lesson number, the fringe trusts `after:`, and the next learner, or the same learner after a reorder, can be offered the line before the one it rests on. `LESSON_PATH_MISMATCH` checks the other direction only, that a built lesson's `data-introduces` equals its line's `terms:` and its first `data-teaches` value equals its line's `tool:` (quoted).
2. **The author has no list to accept or reject.** Evaluation 16 §4.3 item 2 asked for "for each tracked term, the lesson that introduces it and the lessons that use it, hence candidate `after:` lines the path does not yet declare" (quoted); spec 17 D5 deferred it because it needs a scan of every lesson's prose through `proseOf`, a module-level function without `export`, "plus an output format the author accepts line by line" (quoted); spec 20 D0 kept `proseOf` unexported for its own pass and D6 named this one (quoted). The open question 2026-11-01-can-prerequisite-edges-between-teach-terms asks whether introduce-before-use order across a workspace's lessons is enough to reconstruct the prerequisite edges a path author would write, and says what would answer it: build term to introducing lesson and lesson to terms used, derive the candidates, and have the author mark each accept or reject. The listing is the first half; nothing prints it today.
3. **A stall has no signal to read.** `teachIndex` computes `reviewFailed` as `active && lastAttempt?.wrongHere === true`, the last attempt only (quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-19-lines-at-research-vault); a repeated failure on one record is not derived anywhere. The vcluster workspace has no `attempts/` directory at da40b63 (experiment 2026-11-01-probe-what-built-vcluster-lessons-introduce, P5), so no definition of a stall could have fired on the vcluster workspace; evaluation 16 §5 item 2 placed `near:` "after one live mission on the fringe rule has been observed" (quoted in spec 17 D5), and spec 17 D5 says it needs a stall signal that is new derivation over the attempt log (quoted).

## Design

### D0. Contracts the implementation keeps

1. **Read-only.** `teach path edges` writes nothing: not `PATH.md`, not a lesson, not a record. The author edits `PATH.md` by hand after reading the output.
2. **The workspace is the only source.** The inputs are `readPath(ws)` and, for each built line, its lesson's HTML under `lessons/`; no vault path is resolved, no manifest is read, no file outside the workspace is opened. A line whose `lesson:` names no file under `lessons/` (a parked lesson, or a missing one) contributes no use.
3. **Every proposal names its evidence.** A printed proposal carries the line to edit, the `after:` value to add, who uses the term or tool (the lesson id for a built line, `title` for an unbuilt one), the term or tool, and the line that introduces or teaches it with that line's lesson id when it has one.
4. **Pure core.** `pathEdges({ lines, uses })` in `bin/lib/lesson.mjs` takes `readPath`'s array and a `Map` from line number to the uses D2 defines, and returns D3's proposals; it reads no file. `lessonUses(html, terms)` in the same file is the one new reader of lesson prose: it takes `const { blocks, quiz } = parseLesson(html)` and returns the subset of `terms` that `termRe` finds in `proseOf(blocks, quiz)`, so `proseOf` and `termRe` stay module-level and the term rule's reading of a lesson is reused rather than copied (the lint's rule is quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-19-lines-at-research-vault). The command in `bin/commands/teach.mjs` gathers the inputs and formats the lines, as `candidates` does with `pathCandidates`.
5. **Nothing else changes.** `teach next`, `teach lint`'s findings and exit codes, `teach path candidates`, `teach progress`, `teach serve`, the session brief, `readPath`'s shape, `pathFringe`, `lintPath`, `lintPathTerms`, `pathCandidates`, `commandTools`, `taughtTools`, `introducedTerms`, the manifest and the ledger are untouched. No new frontmatter field, no new `PATH.md` field (D5 says why `near:` waits).

### D1. The verb: `teach path edges [workspace]`

The usage string becomes `usage: research-vault teach record add|supersede ... | attempt add ... | lint [--fix] [workspace] | next [workspace] | path candidates [--topic a,b] [workspace] | path edges [workspace] | progress [workspace] | serve [workspace]` (today's string and the `USAGE` constant of `test/teach.test.mjs` that restates it are quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-19-lines-at-research-vault). The workspace rule is the other verbs': the positional path, else the cwd, either holding `MISSION.md`, else usage and exit 2 (`workspace(pos)`, the dispatch and the REFERENCE teach row's statement of the rule are quoted, the first in the snippet's round 2 additions). No flags. A workspace with no `PATH.md` prints `edges: none (write PATH.md)` and exits 0, the shape of `pathLines`'s first empty state.

### D2. Introducers and uses

Let the **introducers** be, for every line `Y` of `PATH.md`, each of its `terms:` values (lowercased as `readPath` returns them) and its `tool:` value. On a lint-clean workspace a built line's `terms:` equals its lesson's `data-introduces` and its `tool:` equals the lesson's first `data-teaches` value (`LESSON_PATH_MISMATCH`, quoted), so the line is read and the lesson's attributes are not parsed a second time; an unbuilt line's `terms:` and `tool:` are the only statement of what it will introduce, and the probe shows the vcluster path's five such terms all sit on unbuilt lines 4, 5 and 9 (experiment 2026-11-01-probe-what-built-vcluster-lessons-introduce, P3). Known terms are introducers only when a line's `terms:` names one, which this spec does not expect (the Floor needs no lesson); on the vcluster path no line introduces a Known term (experiment 2026-11-01-probe-what-built-vcluster-lessons-introduce, P6). A term or tool on two lines takes the lower-numbered line as its introducer.

Let the **uses** of a line `X` be:

- for a built line, every introducer term that `lessonUses(html, terms)` finds in the lesson's prose (the blocks `proseOf` reads: every block but `sources` and `followup`, with `<pre>` and `<script>` removed, plus the quiz text; quoted), each marked `uses`, and every tool `commandTools(blocks)` returns (the first word of each pipe, `&&` or `;` segment of each `$ ` line in `example` and of each command line in `step`, skipping `VAR=value` prefixes and tokens with a path separator; quoted), each marked `runs`;
- for an unbuilt line, every introducer whose `topicSlug` is a substring of `topicSlug(X.title)` (the `slug(t)` rule spec 20 D2 defines and D3 uses for grouping, quoted in the snippet's round 2 additions; `topicSlug` quoted), each marked `names`. The title is the only text an unbuilt line has; on the vcluster path this matches `vcluster` in the titles of lines 5 and 9 (P6, titles).

A line's own introducers are not its uses (line 2 uses `synced resource`, which it introduces). The probe's numbers for the vcluster workspace: lesson 0001 uses no introducer and runs `helm`, `kubectl` and `kind`, none of which a line teaches; lesson 0002 uses its own two terms; lesson 0003 runs its own `vcluster` and uses none of line 2's terms (P6, term and tool use).

### D3. Proposals and the rule for what is printed

Let `reach(X)` be the set of lines `X.after` reaches transitively, over values that name a line (`floor` and a value naming no line contribute nothing; `lintPath` reports the latter, quoted). For every line `X` and every use `(term, Y)` of `X` with `Y` its introducer:

- if `Y` is `X`, or `Y` is in `reach(X)`, nothing: the edge is written or implied;
- if `Y.n < X.n` and `Y` is not in `reach(X)`, one proposal: `X` gains `after: Y.n`;
- if `Y.n > X.n`, nothing is proposed, because `after:` may name earlier lines only (`PATH_AFTER_FORWARD`, quoted) and the author's fix is a reorder; for a built line the lint's term and tool rules as quoted key on lesson number, so this case is usually already a `LESSON_TERM_NOT_INTRODUCED` or `LESSON_TOOL_NOT_INTRODUCED` finding when the Floor and Known tools sections exist, and for an unbuilt title it is a planning signal this spec leaves to the author's reading of `PATH.md` (D5).

One proposal is printed per `(X, Y, term)`, under `X`'s line formatted as `pathLines` formats a line (`fmt`, quoted), so the author sees the current `after:` beside the proposed value; lines print in file order, proposals within a line by `Y.n` then term. The proposal line is `  after: <Y.n> | <lesson id of X, or title> <uses|runs|names> "<term>" | line <Y.n> <introduces|teaches> it (<lesson id of Y, or untaken>)`. The header is `edges for <workspace> | built <lesson numbers> | proposals <n>`; with no proposal the body is one line, `edges: none`.

```
edges for /home/u/vcluster | built 0001, 0002, 0003, 0006 | proposals 1
6. Standalone: the control plane on real machines, no host cluster | after: 1 | lesson: 0006-standalone
  after: 2 | 0006-standalone uses "synced resource" | line 2 introduces it (0002-what-the-syncer-copies)
```

The example is the shape of the output, not a prediction: at da40b63 the vcluster workspace would print `edges: none` (the P6 replica proposes nothing), and the first live run with a proposal to accept or reject is whichever later lesson first uses a term across a missing edge. The proposal is a hard prerequisite in the sense `after:` already has: the lesson's prose uses a term the learner is only taught on that other line, so taking this line first would hit the term undefined. The author may still reject it (the use may be a passing mention the lesson explains inline), which is why it is printed and not written; the open question's second half, the author's accept-or-reject pass and its precision, is recorded in the workspace's `GATE-LOG.md` at each run (D4).

### D5. Not done here

No `near:` field, and no stall derivation. The trigger evaluation 16 §4.3 item 3 named is the review-failed signal repeating on the same record, which would be read from the attempt log as the same record wrong twice running, spec 17 D5's words (`teachIndex` keeps `lastAttempt` only; both quoted); the vcluster workspace has no `attempts/` directory at da40b63 (P5), so it has produced no signal of any definition, and this spec keeps evaluation 16 §5 item 2's placement, "after one live mission on the fringe rule has been observed" (quoted in spec 17 D5), read here as: after a mission has produced a stall signal to read. When one is, `near:` is its own spec: the derivation over the attempt log, a `near:` field `readPath` parses and `lintPath` checks as it checks `after:`, a `near:` line in `teach next` for a stalled record's lesson, and skill text. No writing of `PATH.md`, `NOTES.md` or any file. No vault read: the pass is over the workspace, and spec 20's pass is over the vault. No proposal for a use whose introducer is a later line (D3's third case) and no reorder proposal. No questioning of written `after:` values: an edge the lessons do not support (line 3's `after: 2` on the vcluster path, P6) is the author's and stays. No change to `teach next`, `teach lint`'s findings or exit codes, `teach path candidates`, `pathFringe`, `lintPath`, `lintPathTerms`, `pathCandidates`, `readPath`'s shape, `commandTools`, `taughtTools`, `introducedTerms`, `proseOf`'s export status, the session brief, `LESSON-FORMAT.md`, `GLOSSARY.md` or the fixture lessons. No `--json`. No measurement beyond the `GATE-LOG.md` record of each run's proposals and the author's decisions, which is the open question's second half and accumulates one mission at a time.
