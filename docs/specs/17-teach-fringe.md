# Spec 17: teach fringe, `teach next` offers every PATH.md line the learner is ready for

Round 2 (2026-11-01), on master at 98ed43e (the branch and the lines of evaluation 16, the repo rules and the tests this spec names are quoted in snippet 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at). This is item 1 of evaluation 16's ranked plan (`docs/specs/16-graphify-evaluation.md` §4.3 and §5, quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at): keep the prerequisite graph at the granularity `PATH.md` already has, one line per planned lesson with `after:` naming the lines it needs, and make the teach commands enforce and walk it. The pedagogy is as the captured note on knowledge space theory reports it: a learner's state is the set of items mastered, the outer fringe is every item whose addition is still a feasible state, the inner fringe every item whose removal is, the outer fringe "tells us exactly what the student is ready to learn", and a hand-written prerequisite list is the partial-order approximation of such a structure (2026-11-01-outer-fringe-learning-space-ready-learn; synthesis 2026-11-01-graph-helps-learner-by-exposing-ready). Today's behaviour of `teach next`, `teach lint` and `readPath` on the inputs this spec changes was probed and recorded (experiments 2026-11-01-probe-teach-next-teach-lint-ignore at aa188e6 and 2026-11-01-probe-teach-next-teach-lint-at at 98ed43e); the repo lines this spec relies on are quoted at 98ed43e in the snippets 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17, 2026-11-01-spec-17-companion-lines-at-research and 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at, and the teach files are byte-identical to aa188e6, so the earlier snippet 2026-11-01-research-vault-aa188e6-lines-graphify-evaluation also holds. This spec is the executable list for the implementation; nothing here is implemented at 98ed43e.

## Decisions this spec designs within

Taken in evaluation 16 (§4.3 and its "What not to build" paragraph, quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at) and not reopened here: the graph is the `PATH.md` line graph, authored by the path author, and nothing enumerates states or builds a concept graph over the vault's prose; a sideways relation (`near:`) is a separate field offered only on a stall; and, from the captured note on blocked learners rather than from evaluation 16, `after:` means a hard pedagogical prerequisite and nothing else (2026-11-01-prerequisite-only-paths-block-learners-practitioners); machine-proposed prerequisites are proposed, never written, following the expert-acceptance pattern the educational-graph note reports (2026-11-01-educational-knowledge-graphs-building-prerequisite-relations); the learner sees the fringe, not the graph (the captured note on blocked learners: show the learner the fringe rather than the whole graph; 2026-11-01-prerequisite-only-paths-block-learners-practitioners); and the repo's own rule, "ESM only, no dependencies, no classes" (`AGENTS.md`, quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at).

Two consequences made explicit here so they are not read as drift. First, the inner fringe is defined on the line graph, not on term use: a taken line that no other taken line names in `after:`. Evaluation 16 §4.3 worded it as "the most recently taken lines whose terms no later lesson has used yet" (quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at); the two coincide under one assumption this spec makes explicit, that every line whose lesson uses a term names that term's line, directly or through its chain, in `after:` (the format's rule orders lines, "every \"New to me\" term appears on a line before any line that needs it", and says nothing about `after:`; snippet 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17), and the term-use reading needs a scan of every lesson's prose, which is the deferred candidate pass (D5). Second, review lines are not items: in knowledge space theory the state is made of things mastered, and a review of lines 1 to 3 masters nothing new, so the inner fringe is computed over the taken lines whose lesson is not a review lesson; the outer fringe is computed over every line, so a review line is offered when the lines it reviews are taken.

## Problems

1. `teach next` chooses by file order, not readiness. `pathLine` in `bin/commands/teach.mjs` returns `path.find((x) => !x.lesson)`, the first line with no `lesson:`, and prints its `after:` without reading it (snippets 2026-11-01-research-vault-aa188e6-lines-graphify-evaluation and 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17). Probed at aa188e6: with line 1 declaring `after: 2` and both lines untaken, `teach next` chose line 1 (2026-11-01-probe-teach-next-teach-lint-ignore). Probed at 98ed43e: on a path where lines 2 and 3 both declare `after: 1` and line 1 is the only taken line, it printed `path: 2. Left branch | terms: left | after: 1` and never mentioned line 3, which is equally ready; on a path where line 2 declares `after: 3` and line 3 declares `after: 2`, it printed `path: 2. Needs three | terms: left | after: 3` (2026-11-01-probe-teach-next-teach-lint-at). The skill's §4 step 1 tells the agent to "build the `path:` line" naming "the first `PATH.md` line no lesson has taken" (snippet 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17), so the agent follows the same rule.
2. No shape of `after:` is a lint finding. `readPath` in `bin/lib/lesson.mjs` lowercases each `after:` value and keeps it as a string (`'floor'`, `'1'`) without checking that a number names a line (snippet 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17); probed at 98ed43e, `readPath` on a path with `after: Floor`, `after: 3` on line 2, `after: 2` on line 3, `after: 9` with no line 9 and `after: 5` on line 5 returned `[[1,["floor"],"0001-good"],[2,["3"],null],[3,["2"],null],[4,["9"],null],[5,["5"],null]]`, and `teach lint` on that workspace, run against the test suite's fixture vault with the two legacy records removed, printed `lint: clean` and exited 0 (2026-11-01-probe-teach-next-teach-lint-at). `lintWorkspace` has one path finding, the warning `WARN_PATH_MISSING` for lessons with no `PATH.md` (snippet 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17).
3. The review lesson has no pool beyond spacing. The skill's §6 says a review lesson draws "items drawn from two or three records" and that `review-due` and `review-failed` records "are candidates for a review lesson"; `LESSON-FORMAT.md` says to draw "from two or three learning records once the workspace has at least two, and from the one record before that" (snippet 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17). Neither the §6 bullets nor LESSON-FORMAT's review paragraph names the inner fringe, which the knowledge-space note proposes as the pool a review lesson should draw on (2026-11-01-outer-fringe-learning-space-ready-learn); this spec reads it, on the line graph, as the lessons most recently built on which nothing yet depends.
4. The format text says the old rule. `PATH-FORMAT.md` line 3 says "`teach next` prints the first line no lesson has taken" and its `after:` field says only "lists prerequisite line numbers, or `floor`" (snippet 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17).

## Design

### D0. Contracts the implementation keeps

1. **The fringe is a function of the parsed path.** `pathFringe(lines)` in `bin/lib/lesson.mjs` takes the array `readPath` returns and reads no file; `lintPath(lines)` likewise. Both are testable from an inline array, and `teach next` and `teach lint` call `readPath` once each and pass the result, so the two commands cannot disagree about what a line's `after:` means.
2. **Taken means `lesson:` present.** A line is taken when `readPath` gives it a `lesson`, which includes a parked line (its `lesson:` keeps the id with `parked` appended, PATH-FORMAT line 29, and `readPath` takes the first comma-separated value; snippets 2026-11-01-spec-17-companion-lines-at-research and 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17). Nothing reads the lesson files, the records or the attempts to decide readiness; the path author's `lesson:` is the state. Records and attempts keep their roles in `review-due` and `review-failed`.
3. **`floor` and an absent `after:` are satisfied.** `readPath` gives a line with no `after:` an empty array, and every-of-nothing is true, so such a line is ready; this is stated in PATH-FORMAT (D4) rather than linted, because the first line of the template and of the fixture is this case, as is line 1 of every path that lints clean under D2.
4. **A value that names no line is never satisfied.** The line stays out of the fringe until the author fixes it, and `PATH_AFTER_FORWARD` names the value (D2). The conservative reading, because an unknown prerequisite might be a line the author forgot to write.
5. **Nothing else changes.** `readPath`'s output shape, `LESSON_PATH_MISMATCH` (snippet 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at), the records table and the `review-due:` and `review-failed:` lines of `teach next`, `teach progress`, `teach serve` (the usage string, quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at) and the session brief are untouched; `WARN_PATH_MISSING` stays as it is.

### D1. `teach next`: the `fringe:` lines and the `inner:` lines

`pathFringe(lines)` returns `{ fringe, inner }`:

- `fringe`: every untaken line whose every `after:` value is `floor` or the number of a taken line, in file order. The first entry is the default.
- `inner`: every taken line whose lesson is not a review lesson (its id does not have `review-` after the four-digit number, the test that `teachIndex` and `lessonClass` apply; snippet 2026-11-01-spec-17-companion-lines-at-research) and that no other such line names in `after:`, ordered by lesson id descending, so the most recently built lesson comes first.

`pathLine` in `bin/commands/teach.mjs` becomes `pathLines(ws)` and returns an array of lines in place of the one `path:` line, with today's two empty states (`none (write PATH.md)`, `complete`) carried over under the new name and one new one for a path whose untaken lines all wait:

```
fringe: none (write PATH.md)                        PATH.md absent (readPath returns null); no inner: line
fringe: complete                                    every line taken
fringe: none (every untaken line waits on an untaken or unknown line; run teach lint)
fringe: 2. Left branch | terms: left | after: 1     one line per ready line, file order, first is the default
fringe: 3. Right branch | terms: right | after: 1
inner: 1. What fx returns | terms: fx | after: floor | lesson: 0001-good
inner: none                                         no taken non-review line
```

Each `fringe:` and `inner:` line is the PATH.md line reformatted as `N. <title>` followed by the fields present, in the order `terms`, `tool`, `after`, `lesson`, joined by ` | `, which is today's `path:` format with `lesson:` appended for taken lines. On the fixture path as extended in D4 (lines 1 to 3 taken, 4 and 5 both `after: 1`, 6 `after: 4, 5`), the last lines of `teach next` are:

```
fringe: 4. The sextuple, named | terms: sextuple | after: 1
fringe: 5. The septuple, named | terms: septuple | after: 1
inner: 2. A bad lesson | after: 1 | lesson: 0002-bad
```

Line 3 is the review `0003-review-good` and is not an item, so line 2, which no other non-review taken line names, is the inner fringe; line 1 is named by line 2 and is not.

### D2. `teach lint`: `PATH_AFTER_FORWARD` and `PATH_AFTER_CYCLE`

`lintPath(lines)` returns findings with `file: 'PATH.md'`, both violations (not `WARN_`), so `teach lint` exits 1 on either as it does on every other violation (snippet 2026-11-01-spec-17-companion-lines-at-research):

- `PATH_AFTER_FORWARD`, one per offending value: an `after:` value that is not `floor` and is not the number of a line in the path (`line 4 after: 9 names no line`), or that is the number of a line at or after itself (`line 2 after: 3 names a later line`; `line 5 after: 5 names itself`). Line numbers are the `N.` prefixes `readPath` parses, compared as numbers, so the rule is "a prerequisite has a smaller number", which is also what makes the path readable top to bottom.
- `PATH_AFTER_CYCLE`, one per cycle: a depth-first walk over the `after:` edges between lines that exist, reporting each cycle once as `lines 2 -> 3 -> 2 form an after: cycle`, on the line with the smallest number in it. With numbers totally ordered, every cycle contains at least one forward edge, so a cycle is always also a `PATH_AFTER_FORWARD`; both codes are kept because the fixes differ (reorder or renumber for a forward edge; break the loop for a cycle) and the loop is what the author needs to see.

`lintWorkspace` pushes `lintPath(readPath(ws))` when `readPath` is not null, before the sort it already applies; `WARN_PATH_MISSING` keeps its branch. A finding carries the line's number, so `teach lint` output names what to edit.

### D3. The skill: pick from the fringe, review from the inner fringe

`skills/research-teach/SKILL.md` §4 step 1 changes from "the `path:` line naming the first `PATH.md` line no lesson has taken ... Otherwise build the `path:` line" to: read the `fringe:` lines, one per `PATH.md` line whose prerequisites are all taken, and the `inner:` lines; records in `review-due` or `review-failed` still come first, as a review lesson; otherwise build a line from the fringe, the first unless the learner or the records say otherwise (a fringe line whose terms the records show the learner already uses, or one the learner asks for); `fringe: none (every untaken line waits ...)` means fix `PATH.md` first, and `teach lint` names the line. The sentence that a lesson the path did not plan gets a line added first stays.

§6, the review bullet: a review lesson draws its two or three records first from `review-due` and `review-failed`, then from the records of the lessons the `inner:` lines name, most recent first, on the design's working assumption that the lessons nothing has built on yet are the ones whose records have had the least retrieval since. The spacing bullet is unchanged. `LESSON-FORMAT.md`'s review paragraph is unchanged (it says how many records and that each item names one; the pool is the skill's to say).

### D4. PATH-FORMAT, the fixture and the couplings

`PATH-FORMAT.md`: line 3 becomes "`teach next` prints the fringe: every line whose `after:` lines are all built, the first being the default, and the inner fringe: the most recently built lessons nothing depends on yet". The `after:` field gains: numbers name earlier lines only; a line with no `after:` is ready from the start, as `floor` is; `teach lint` reports `PATH_AFTER_FORWARD` for a value naming a later, missing or own line and `PATH_AFTER_CYCLE` for a loop. The "Reorder freely" rule gains one sentence: renumbering changes what `after:` means, so run `teach lint` after a reorder.

`test/fixtures/teach/PATH.md` gains two lines, so the fixture path branches and joins:

```
5. The septuple, named | terms: septuple | after: 1
6. Join of 4 and 5 | after: 4, 5
```

No lesson, record or mission text changes: `trackedTerms`'s comment says it tracks the Floor's "New to me" plus every lesson's `data-introduces`, not `PATH.md` (snippet 2026-11-01-research-vault-aa188e6-lines-graphify-evaluation), untaken lines carry no lesson for the lesson lint to check, and `readPath`'s fourth element, which `test/lesson.test.mjs` asserts (quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at), is unchanged; that test's line count moves from 4 to 6.

`scripts/deps.mjs` `COUPLINGS` (the array and its `[from, to, why]` row shape are quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at): the three existing rows between `bin/lib/lesson.mjs`, `bin/commands/teach.mjs`, `PATH-FORMAT.md` and `SKILL.md` (snippet 2026-11-01-research-vault-98ed43e-lines-spec-17) have their third, `why`, strings extended to name the `fringe:` and `inner:` lines and the two lint codes; no new row, since the files already couple.

### D5. Not done here

No `near:` field: a sideways move needs a stall signal, and `teachIndex` computes `reviewFailed` as `active && lastAttempt?.wrongHere === true`, the last attempt only (quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at), so "the same record wrong twice running" is new derivation over the attempt log plus a new field, a new `teach next` line and skill text; evaluation 16 §5 placed it "after one live mission on the fringe rule has been observed" (quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at), and the cost confirms it is not a few lines. No term-prerequisite candidate pass (`teach path candidates`): it needs a scan of every lesson's prose for every tracked term, which the lesson lint does today (`bin/lib/lesson.mjs` lines 311-316 at 98ed43e) through `proseOf`, a module-level function without `export` (quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at), plus an output format the author accepts line by line; it answers 2026-11-01-can-prerequisite-edges-between-teach-terms and is its own spec. No readiness from records or attempts: `lesson:` is the state (D0 rule 2). No change to `readPath`'s shape, to duplicate line numbers (`readPath` does not check for them, and neither does this spec), to `LESSON_PATH_MISMATCH`, to the session brief, to `teach progress` or `teach serve`, to `docs/REFERENCE.md`'s teach row (which lists the verbs and their arguments, not `teach next`'s output; quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at), or to `LESSON-FORMAT.md`. No measurement: 2026-11-01-does-choosing-next-lesson-from-prerequisite stays open until a live mission has run on the fringe rule. No change to `check`'s prose rows (evaluation 16 §6 records that `check.mjs` does not consult status for prose citations; quoted in 2026-11-01-spec-17-round-2-lines-at; its fix is outside this spec).
