## Issue E. Figure text that spills out of its shape: an advisory lint on estimated text width

What exists. The figure lint (`figureFindings`, bin/lib/lesson.mjs lines 311 to 342, quoted in 2026-11-03-lines-behind-2026-11-03-rendering) warns on a fixed `width` or `height` attribute, a viewBox wider than `FIGURE_VIEWBOX_MAX` (440, line 17), a missing `role="img"` or `aria-label`, a fill or stroke that is not `currentColor`, `none` or `var(--muted)`, a label under `FIGURE_LABEL_MIN` (14, line 18) units and a label over `FIGURE_LABEL_MAX` (40, line 19) characters. Nothing in it relates a `<text>` element's position and length to the `<rect>` it sits in or to the viewBox width. In the learner's lesson 0002 (the quoted `grep -o` block), the first figure's cluster box is `<rect x="290" y="10" width="140" ...>` and holds `<text x="300" y="54">kubernetes.default.svc</text>`, 22 characters at the svg's `font-size="14"` (both svg open tags are quoted in full); the second figure's health box holds `<text x="235" y="80">Healthy, Progressing, Degraded</text>`, 30 characters, and the svg's viewBox is 440 wide. The svg tags set `font-size="14"` and neither `<text>` sets its own; both labels are under 40 characters. The learner's screenshot shows "kubernetes.default.sv" cut at the box edge and "Healthy, Progressing, Degraded" past the border (the quoted report), and wrote "Diagrams often have texts spilling out of the shapes."

What remains. An advisory `WARN_FIGURE_TEXT_OVERFLOW`, in `figureFindings` beside the label checks: for each `<text>` with numeric `x` and `y`, estimate its width as characters times font size times 0.5 (judgment: an average advance for a sans-serif face; the aim is to catch the cases above, not to measure, and 0.5 rather than 0.55 so that "namespace default" in the same box, which the screenshot shows fitting, is not flagged), place it by `text-anchor` (`middle` centres on `x`, `end` ends at `x`, else starts at `x`), find the `<rect>` in the same svg whose box contains the point (`x`, `y`), and warn when the text's right edge passes the rect's right edge less 4 units, or its left edge passes the rect's left edge plus 4, or, with no containing rect, when it passes the viewBox's width. One finding per figure, as `WARN_FIGURE_LABEL_LONG` gives, naming the labels. Advisory, since the estimate is a heuristic and a `<tspan>` layout or a narrower face can be fine. The fix for an author is a wider box, a shorter label or a line break. One test beside the figure tests at test/lesson.test.mjs lines 384 to 392: the fixture figure with its `fx` label (at x=100, inside the rect `<rect x="70" y="15" width="60" height="30" .../>`, quoted) replaced by "the function fx" warns; the fixture as it is does not.

Done when: the warning exists with that rule, the two labels above would trigger it (checked by the test's arithmetic, since the argocd lesson is not a fixture), `LESSON-FORMAT.md`'s model-block section (lines 67 to 81, quoted) names it, `npm test` passes and `CHANGELOG.md` has the line.

## Issue F. Prose fills the block, and a paired figure floats with the text wrapping around it

What exists. `lesson.css` keeps every paragraph and list inside a block to a 65-character measure: "Prose keeps a 65ch measure inside any block; a figure is not prose and spans." and `[data-block] :is(p, ul, ol) { max-width: 65ch; }` (lines 67 and 68, quoted in 2026-11-03-lines-behind-2026-11-03-rendering); the body is capped at `max-width: 90rem` (line 32). A `.pair` is a one-column grid (line 105) that becomes two equal columns from 1100px (line 146), the paragraph left and the figure right, and a figure's svg has `min-width: 27.5rem` (line 101). Spec 22's problem 6 quotes reading guidance on line length (WCAG's 80 characters, Baymard's 50 to 75, Butterick's 45 to 90; docs/specs/22-lesson-shapes.md line 16), and the format's pair line says the stylesheet "lays out as two columns on a wide screen and stacks on a narrow one" (LESSON-FORMAT.md line 71). The learner's second screenshot shows the mission block's paragraph stopping at about half the block's width with the right half empty, and the model block's paragraph in the left column beside the figure; they wrote "I don't think it makes sense for text to not dynamically adjust to the page. if anything, there should be buffer space around the image where the text breaks up but the text and still fill the page horizontally" (the quoted report).

What remains. The learner's call overrides the measure, and this issue records that: drop line 68 so prose fills its block at every width. From 1100px, `.pair` stops being a grid and its figure floats right with a buffer, `float: right; width: 45%; margin: 0 0 1rem 1.5rem`, with the pair clearing its float after; below 1100px the figure stacks as now. A float wraps only the content that follows it, so the pair's order changes: the format's line 71 becomes `<div class="pair"><figure>…</figure><p>…</p></div>`, the figure first, "which the stylesheet floats right on a wide screen so the paragraph wraps around it, and stacks above the paragraph on a narrow one"; the stylesheet comments on lines 67 and 104 change with the rules; the skill's line that shows the pair follows the format. The aim, to be checked by the Done-when rendering, is that the paragraph wraps around the figure and continues full width below it. An advisory `WARN_PAIR_FIGURE_LAST` names a pair whose `<p>` comes before its `<figure>`, so an existing lesson (every pair built so far has the paragraph first) is reordered at its next build rather than silently losing the wrap; until then its figure floats after the paragraph. The figure's own rules (line 101's `min-width` and the `overflow-x: auto` on line 100) stay. One check: a test that `lesson.css` has no `65ch` rule and has the float rule inside the 1100px media query, beside the reading-page test at test/teach-store.test.mjs lines 357 to 366 (quoted), and one lint test for the warning.

Done when: the rules are in `lesson.css` with the comments, the format's line 71 and the skill's pair line put the figure first, the warning exists, the tests pass, a copy of the learner's lesson 0002 with its pairs reordered, rendered at 1100px or wider, shows the model paragraph wrapping around the floated figure and the mission paragraph filling its block (a rendering captured as an experiment before the issue closes), `npm test` passes and `CHANGELOG.md` has the line. Existing workspaces take the new stylesheet the way issue #53's What remains says (quoted: delete the workspace copy before the next build so `cp -n` delivers the new one).
