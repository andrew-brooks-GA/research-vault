## Decisions

### D0. Contracts kept

1. No new lint, as the issue decided: whether a sentence states an idea is the reader's judgment. The expectation that `teach lint` and `npm test` stay clean on the edited fixtures is checked in Execution, not asserted here.
2. The term rule is untouched. The mission block is in `proseOf`, so a lead must avoid tracked terms the lesson may not use: by the quoted `trackedTerms` and `introducedTerms`, `sextuple` in every fixture, while `fx` is allowed in 0001 and the later lessons. The lint-clean test in Execution is the check. The issue's rule that the lead describes the new thing without naming this lesson's `data-introduces` term is the author's rule, kept by the sentences in D2.
3. Review and `project` lessons are exempt, as the issue decided; 0003 and 0006 are not edited. 0001 has no `data-shape` and is therefore a `concept` lesson (format line 11), the case the issue said had no fixture; it gets the idea sentence too, and so does the format's example page, whose mission block matches it.
4. The issue says an edited `mission` unit needs a new claims-gate verdict. The three edited lessons are re-recorded MERIT without a reviewer on the ground of the two 2026-11-01 decisions (0001's and the shaped fixtures'), test data citing the ids `fixtureVault()` writes, with a new GATE-LOG entry naming this spec; `gate status <file> --check` on each is in the Execution order, since `gate staged --check` covers only `docs/**/*.md`.

### D1. The format

`LESSON-FORMAT.md` line 23 becomes: "One sentence tying the win to `MISSION.md`; in a `concept`, `tool` or `diagnose` lesson one stating the idea, what is true about the subject, in terms allowed before this lesson's `data-introduces` and without naming the new term; then in a shaped lesson one naming the activity ("you will complete the command yourself"). Review and `project` lessons carry no idea sentence." The Shapes section gains one sentence after the table: the idea sentence is the page's lead, what the reader can say the page teaches after the mission block, distinct from the win (what they can do afterwards) and the activity (what they do on the page). The example page's mission line (line 163) gains 0001's idea sentence from D2.
